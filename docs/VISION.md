# Vision

## The problem

People who work across several organizations live in several inboxes, several Slack or Teams workspaces, and several calendars. No single client sees all of it, so nothing can tell you that a meeting in one account overlaps a meeting in another, that a request in one inbox was already answered in a chat somewhere else, or what you promised someone last week.

## What Station is

A self-hosted web app that:

1. Syncs every account you have into one local store.
2. Groups them into **Orbits**, one per company, client, or area of life.
3. Shows **Mission Control**: a single view of what needs you today, across every Orbit.
4. Uses an AI model, with tight limits, to extract what matters: requests, commitments, deadlines, and context for meetings.
5. Gives you **CAPCOM**, a way to ask questions across everything and get cited answers.

## What Station is not

- **Not a mail server.** Your mail stays with your provider. Station is a client.
- **Not an autonomous agent.** It never sends, deletes, books, or posts on its own. A user may opt into evidence-backed internal task completion with undo.
- **Not a hosted service.** There is no Station cloud. Each install is independent.
- **Not a team tool (in v1).** One user per install. Multi-user may come later.

## Vocabulary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Mission Control** | The overview screen across all Orbits |
| **Orbit** | A named group of accounts plus context, filters, and privacy rules |
| **CAPCOM** | Ask mode: plain-language questions answered with citations |
| **Flight Plan** | A proposed plan for the day that fits tasks around meetings |
| **Morning Briefing** | The daily summary at the top of Mission Control |
| **Blackout** | Focus mode. Holds notifications from other Orbits |
| **Docking** | Connecting an account ("Dock a new account") |
| **Review tray** | Where AI-proposed tasks and changes wait for your approval |
| **Promises** | Commitments you made and things you are waiting on from others |

Use the theme in names and headings only. Buttons and menus use plain words: Reply, Archive, Search, Add task.

## Who it's for

Technical people who self-host, juggle several organizations, and want an assistant that reads across their chosen data and leaves provider actions to them.
